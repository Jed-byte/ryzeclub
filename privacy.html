Who is responsible for your data

Ryze Club, operated by [company name, to be added], decides how your personal data is used. This policy follows the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021).

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address, from Apple, Google or email sign-in.
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Your photo, if you add one.
  • The clubs you follow and the sessions you book, cancel and attend.
  • A record of each payment: amount, session and date. Card details go to Ziina or Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Your reminder choices.
  • Your location, only if you allow it, to show clubs near you.
  • For hosts: your club details, schedule, cover photo and payment account connection.

Why we use it

  • To book your sessions, take payments and handle refunds.
  • To show your first name and photo to people at the same session, and to that club's host.
  • To send reminders you have switched on.
  • To keep the app working and safe, and to fix problems.

Who can see it

  • People at your session: your first name and photo.
  • Hosts of clubs you book with: your name, bookings and attendance for their club.
  • Payment providers: what they need to take the payment.
  • Service providers who host the app or send messages for us, under contract.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show you ads.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your data is stored in a cloud region inside the UAE. We keep it while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photo and follows within 30 days. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as UAE law requires.

Your rights

  • See the data we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile, then Delete my account.
  • Withdraw consent for reminders or location at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
