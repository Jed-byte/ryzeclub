Privacy policy

Draft. Ryze Club is a prototype. This policy has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be checked against the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021) before launch.

Last updated 10 November 2026

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address, from Google sign-in.
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Anything you choose to add to your profile: photo, area, level, work, interests and Instagram handle.
  • The clubs you follow, the sessions you book and attend, and your streak.
  • Your choices about photos, tagging, standings and reminders.
  • A record of each payment: amount, session and date. Card details go to the payment provider (Ziina or Stripe). Ryze Club never sees or stores them.

Why we use it

  • To book your sessions, show your week and keep your streak.
  • To show your profile to people at the same session, and to that club's organisers, only if you turn sharing on.
  • To send reminders you have switched on.
  • To pay organisers and handle refunds.

Who can see it

  • People at your session: your first name. Your full profile only if you turn sharing on.
  • Organisers of clubs you book with: your name, bookings and attendance for their club, and your profile if sharing is on.
  • Payment providers: what they need to take the payment.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show you ads.

Photos

We ask before your first booking whether you are happy to appear in session photos. If you say no, nobody can tag you, and any photo of you is removed when you ask. Members can only post photos after attending a club five times.

Where it is stored and for how long

To be confirmed before launch: the hosting location, and how long data is kept after an account is deleted. Payment records are kept for as long as UAE law requires.

Your rights

  • See the data we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile, then Delete my account.
  • Withdraw consent for photos, tagging or reminders at any time in Profile.

Contact

A contact email for privacy requests will be published here before launch.
