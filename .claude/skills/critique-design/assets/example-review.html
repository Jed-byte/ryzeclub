What works

Keep these: they are better than the brief

  • The dispute answer. "AED 54 below the minimum spend", then "What to tell the customer" with a Copy button and a ready sentence. This is the commercial heart of the page.
  • The cashback ledger. "Balance = Payable now + On hold" printed beside the bar, plus all four states (current, all zero, all on hold, all paid).
  • The info tips. 103 of them with real definitions, for example "GMV ÷ cashback cost. Shows sales we can prove, not that the offer caused them."
  • The campaign page. "AED 10 in sales per AED 1 of cashback" and "After AED 1,140 in refunds" are exactly the plain English we want. The completed campaign says "All payable · Refund window closed".
  • Day-one states. The Customers, Outlets, Rejections and campaign screens in Flow 1 explain what will appear and when.
  • The filter. "1 of 6", Reset, the "Filtered: 1 of 6 campaigns" line, and the "Brand-wide data" note you designed.
  • The rules from the brief. Only the four reasons a merchant can change, no targeting shown as an error, one bank per campaign, days left instead of dates in the table.

US-06 Campaign filter · screens 3.6 to 3.8 Before approval

Say which sections the campaign filter changes

With "Summer Cashback" selected, the Customers tab shows the funnel for all campaigns next to rejection reasons for Summer only, and nothing tells them apart. A merchant will read the brand-wide funnel as Summer's. The brief's principle 5 asks every section that ignores the filter to say so, and your "Brand-wide data" note already does this on E.2 and E.3.
