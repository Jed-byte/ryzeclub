Who can use Ryze Club

You must be 18 or older. One account per person, in your real first name.

What Ryze Club is

Ryze Club helps you find community sports clubs and book their sessions. Each club is run by its own organiser. Ryze Club does not run the sessions and is not responsible for what happens at them.

Taking part

  • Sport carries a risk of injury. Check you are fit to take part, and tell the organiser about anything they should know.
  • Follow the organiser's instructions and the rules of the venue.

Paying and cancelling

  • Prices are set by each organiser and shown before you pay.
  • Your payment is taken by Ziina or Stripe and goes to the organiser.
  • Cancel up to 12 hours before a session for a full refund. After that, refunds are up to the organiser.
  • If the organiser cancels a session, you get a full refund.

For organisers

  • Ryze Club charges 10% of each booking, and 0% for your first three months.
  • You are responsible for your sessions, your venue permissions and any licence your activity needs.
  • Keep members' details private and use them only to run your club.

How to behave

  • Be respectful in person and in the app.
  • Do not photograph or post anyone who has not agreed to it. In the UAE this can be a criminal offence.
  • We can suspend accounts that break these rules.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile.

Law
