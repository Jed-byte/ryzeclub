Who we are

Ryze Club is an app for finding community clubs and booking their sessions, operated by [company name and licence number, to be added]. In these terms, "we" means Ryze Club, "host" means a person or club that runs sessions, and "member" means someone who books them.

Who can use Ryze Club

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • One account per person, with your real first name and accurate details.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. You are responsible for bookings made from your account.

What Ryze Club does

Hosts list clubs and sessions across Mind (such as chess and book clubs), Body (sport and fitness) and Soul (such as painting and pottery). Members find and book them. Each session is run by its host, not by Ryze Club.

Booking and paying

  • You pay per session, at the price the host sets. It is shown before you book. There are no subscriptions.
  • Payments are taken by Ziina (or Stripe, for some hosts) and go to the host. Card details are handled by the payment provider, not by us.
  • A paid booking is confirmed once the payment provider confirms the payment.
  • Members do not pay Ryze Club a fee. Hosts pay our fee (see "For hosts").

Cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 12 hours before a session for a full refund.
  • Later than that, the host's own policy applies. It is shown on the club page.
  • If a host cancels a session or removes it from the schedule, everyone booked is told and refunded in full.
  • Refunds go back to the way you paid. How long they take depends on your bank.

Taking part

  • Physical activities carry a risk of injury. Check you are fit to take part and tell the host anything they should know.
  • Follow the host's instructions and the rules of the venue.
  • Look after your own belongings.

For hosts

  • Keep your club page, prices and schedule accurate. Confirm or change your sessions when they change.
  • Honour bookings. If you cancel a session, members are refunded in full.
  • Ryze Club charges 10% of each paid booking, and 0% for your first 3 months. The fee is taken from what members pay you.
  • You are responsible for your sessions, venue permission, any licence or permit your activity needs, and suitable insurance.
  • Use members' details only to run your club, and keep them private.
  • Only use photos, including your cover photo, of people who agreed to be in them, and that you have the right to use.

Photos and what you post

  • You keep ownership of photos and text you post. You let us show them in Ryze Club while they are posted.
  • Do not photograph or post anyone who has not agreed to it. In the UAE this can be a criminal offence.
  • Ask us to remove a photo of you and we will.

How to behave

  • Be respectful in person and in the app.
  • No harassment, discrimination, spam, or selling to members without their agreement.
  • We can suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

Our responsibility

Hosts run their own sessions, so we are not responsible for what happens at them. As far as UAE law allows, our liability for a booking is limited to the amount paid for it. Nothing here limits rights you have under UAE consumer law.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will tell you in the app at least 14 days before the change applies.

Ending your account

Delete your account at any time from Profile. Bookings and payment records are kept as UAE law requires.

Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the UAE as applied in Dubai. Disputes go to the courts of Dubai.
