# Competitor review: ClassPass and Mindbody

**Question:** what do ClassPass and Mindbody do well that Ryze Club should take, and where are they weak in ways Ryze Club should point out?

**Evidence:** a 6-minute screen recording the founder made on 19 November 2026 of the ClassPass iPhone app in Dubai, on a free account with 0 credits. The founder could not get into the Mindbody app, so the Mindbody section uses public reviews only. The recording's audio was not transcribed.

## Who they are

- **ClassPass** is the member app. You buy credits monthly and spend them at gyms, studios, spas and salons.
- **Mindbody** is mainly software that studios pay for: timetable, bookings, payments and marketing. It also has a member app for booking at a studio's own prices.
- They are one company. Mindbody bought ClassPass in 2021, and both have sat under a parent brand, Playlist, since June 2025. It reports over 40,000 Mindbody businesses and 73,000 ClassPass partners.

**How they compete with Ryze Club:** they serve paid studios, while Ryze Club serves community clubs that meet in parks, on beaches and on public padel courts. That kind of club is not on ClassPass. The competition is for the same person's week, the habit of opening one app to decide where to train, and the money they spend on it. On the organiser side, Mindbody is what a studio uses, but community organisers use WhatsApp and Instagram, so WhatsApp is the real competitor there.

## Take: what they do well

Ordered by fit with the MVP (D41: find, book and pay per session, about 15 hosts in 3 areas).

| # | Pattern | Ryze Club version | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tap a time straight from search results | Each club card in results shows its next two or three sessions as buttons that open the pay sheet | MVP |
| 2 | Filters for Time, Distance and Spots left | Day, morning or evening, area, and "spots left" when a host sets a cap | MVP |
| 3 | "Ladies Only" tag shown in results | The prototype already has a ladies-only setting for hosts. Show it as a tag on cards and as a filter | MVP |
| 4 | "How to get there" with landmarks | Already built as "How to find us". Make it more visible: show it on the booking confirmation and in the reminder | MVP |
| 5 | "Report inaccurate information" | A small link on each club page that goes to the curator. Supports the "last confirmed" rule | MVP |
| 6 | "No results today, viewing tomorrow" | Never show an empty day: jump to the next day with sessions and say so | MVP |
| 7 | "25+ bookings this week" | People, not counts: "14 going on Saturday" with faces. The pay sheet already does this; add it to cards | MVP |
| 8 | Save, then a nudge to book this week | After Follow: "Pick a session this week" with the next three times | MVP |
| 9 | Amenities | For outdoor clubs: parking, water, toilets, shade, bag drop, showers nearby. In Dubai heat these matter | MVP (host fills in, curator pre-fills) |
| 10 | Invite a friend from the club page | "Bring a friend" link to a specific session, sent by WhatsApp. No credit reward in the MVP | MVP |
| 11 | A cheap first try (AED 5 trial) | "First session free" or a lower first-session price, set by each host. Ask hosts first (parked idea 1) | Later |
| 12 | Map with "Redo search in this area" | Not needed for 3 areas and 15 hosts. Add it when the city opens up, without other businesses' pins | Later |
| 13 | Ratings with reviews naming the instructor | Members thank a host or session after going. Wait for real reviews (D39) | Later |

## Highlight: where they are weak

These are the gaps Ryze Club should be clear about, in product and in marketing.

| # | Their weakness | What we saw or read | Ryze Club's answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pricing is hard to follow** | Credits, a different credit cost per class, AED 349 a month auto-renewing, credits expire, late-cancel and missed-class fees. Late-cancel fees are the most common complaint in reviews | The price in AED, per session, set by the host. Pay only for the session you book. No subscription, no credits, nothing expires. Our own cancel rule must be just as plain |
| 2 | **Paywall before anything else** | "Unlock all businesses" on every screen and "0 credits" in the header. You can look but cannot book until you start a trial | Book any session straight away. No plan to buy first |
| 3 | **You are a customer, not a member** | You fill one of the spots the studio gives ClassPass and meet different strangers each time. Nothing shows who else is going | Who's going, with faces. The same people each week. The host knows your name. Your streak shows you keep turning up |
| 4 | **Too much of everything** | Waxing, lashes, cryotherapy and salons next to boxing. Restaurants and salons on the map | Hand-picked clubs in three pillars, Mind, Body and Soul. Nothing that is not a community |
| 5 | **No outdoor or community clubs** | Studios and gyms only. Run clubs, beach workouts and community padel are not listed | That is all Ryze Club lists |
| 6 | **Retention is content, not people** | A whole tab of recorded treadmill audio and partner videos with US instructors | People bring you back: your club, your streak, the first-timer welcome. Do not copy the Workouts tab |
| 7 | **Picks ignore where you are** | First "Must-try" studio was 21 km away | Home's picks follow D43: soonest session with spots, in your area |
| 8 | **Breaks and leaves the app** | Video opens YouTube. "Add credits" failed. Spinners | Keep paying and booking fast and reliable. Photos stay in the app |
| 9 | **Mindbody is costly and complex for organisers** | Reviews call it expensive and hard to use, with a phone app that needs a laptop for simple tasks (G2 3.7/5) | The organiser app takes under five minutes a week on a phone (D30). The curator pre-fills the page (D32). No monthly software fee: Ryze Club takes its fee per booking |

## Lines to use

Draft wording, to test with members:

- "No credits. No subscription. Pay for the session you go to."
- "Not a class. A club."
- "See who's going before you go."
- For organisers: "Your club's page, bookings and payments. Five minutes a week, from your phone."
