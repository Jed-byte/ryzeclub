# Concept verdict

**Verdict guidance:** `proceed` (on the narrowed concept below)
**Concept strength:** medium

This is guidance for product authors, not a gate.

## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Newcomers to Dubai who want a regular run or padel group. Second user: the club organiser |
| **Problem** | Community clubs live across Instagram, WhatsApp and one-off websites. They are hard to find, and people drop off after a first visit |
| **Changed outcome** | A newcomer finds a suitable club in one sitting, turns up, and has a reason to return. A club has one live page that its members keep fresh |
| **Smallest credible V1** | About 30 hand-picked run and community padel clubs, Dubai-wide, mobile-first website |

## V1 scope

1. Three-question quiz leading to a short list of clubs.
2. Club page with schedule, level and location.
3. Session RSVP with reminders.
4. Photo posts by members and organisers, tagged to a club and session.
5. Attendance streak.

## Not in V1

Coaches, dietitians, 1:1 sessions, payments, supplements, ads, a general social feed, comments, messaging, a native app, credential or licence checks, medical or nutrition advice.

## Success signals (pilot of about 8 weeks)

- At least 10 of the 30 clubs keep their schedule current without being chased.
- At least 30% of newcomers who RSVP once RSVP to a second session within 14 days.
- At least half of sessions have at least one photo from any member.

## Why medium, not strong

- The market and the appetite for community sport are well supported.
- The core bet is unproven: that discovery is a felt pain, and that organisers and members will adopt a shared tool. Interviews were skipped by the founder's choice.
- Member-posted photos reduce the dependence on organisers, which makes the concept more resilient.

## Major unsupported claims

See the claim ledger in `prfaq.md`. The five marked unproven or assumed are the questions the pilot must answer.

## Accepted risks

- Licensing and permits are deferred for V1 by the founder. The platform lists sessions and does not organise them.
- Trust and safety checks beyond report, untag and hide are deferred.

## PRD handoff notes

- Write for two users: newcomer and organiser. The organiser's workload is the make-or-break.
- Model a session so that a host and a price can be added later for coaches and 1:1.
- Make "last confirmed" data and manual curation explicit requirements.
- Carry the unproven claims into the PRD as pilot questions.
