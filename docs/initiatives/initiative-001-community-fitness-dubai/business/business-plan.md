# Ryze Club business plan

*Find your people, keep showing up.* Now for mind, body and soul.

All money is in AED and excludes VAT. USD is at the pegged rate of 3.6725. Every number below comes from `ryze-club-forecast.xlsx` (base scenario) unless a source is named. Change the blue cells on its **Assumptions** tab and every table updates.

## 1. Summary

Ryze Club started as a free directory of Dubai's community sports clubs. Payments now work in the prototype: an organiser connects Ziina or Stripe, sets a fixed price per session, and members pay when they book. Ryze keeps 10% of each booking. This plan treats Ryze Club as a **home for paid communities** in three pillars:

| Pillar | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Mind** | Groups that think and talk together | Book clubs, language exchanges, author talks, writing circles, debate nights |
| **Body** | Groups that move together | Pilates, yoga, padel, tennis, boxing, five-a-side, run clubs |
| **Soul** | Groups that make things together | Supper clubs, cooking classes, pottery, sketching, calligraphy, culture evenings |

One-off **workshops and culture events** sit across all three pillars. Every quarter, a **Ryze Mind Body Soul day** brings the three together: a morning class, a book swap and a long lunch.

**The base case in three years:**

| | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Live paid communities (year end) | 91 | 179 | 276 |
| Value paid through Ryze (GMV) | 3.53M | 10.02M | 18.09M |
| Net revenue | 275k | 1.05M | 2.02M |
| Gross margin | 65% | 80% | 85% |
| Operating profit (EBITDA) | (826k) | (971k) | (706k) |

- Monthly losses shrink from about 75k to 85k in late 2027 to 24k in December 2029. Monthly break-even comes in **early 2030** in the base case (extending the trend beyond the 36 months modelled) and **June 2029** in the high case.
- **Funding needed: about AED 2.5M (USD 680k)** in the base case, or AED 1.5M (USD 400k) in the high case.
- Three levers each bring break-even forward to December 2029: a shorter fee-free period, a 12% fee, or lower community churn (section 8). Together they bring it to **June 2029** and cut the funding need to AED 1.6M.

## 3. The problem

Dubai is a city of newcomers. The Dubai Fitness Challenge drew more than 3 million participants in 2025 (WAM), and people pay for community: a local writer describes Dubai as "a forever transient city" whose residents are "craving connection", and puts paid community events at AED 300 to 400 (*Half-Light*, p. 53).

Finding a group is still hard. Clubs live across Instagram, WhatsApp and one-off websites. Organisers chase payments by bank transfer, run sign-ups in WhatsApp polls, and lose track of who paid. The same is true, often worse, for a book club or a supper club: these have no Playtomic and no ClassPass.

**For members:** one place to find a group that fits, see who is going, pay in two taps, and keep a streak.

**For organisers:** a page that fills itself, bookings that arrive paid, and a payout to their own Ziina or Stripe account. A routine week takes under five minutes (D30).

## 4. The offer by pillar

The forecast models each pillar as recurring paid communities. The numbers are starting assumptions to test with hosts.

| | Body | Mind | Soul | Workshop (one-off) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Price per session | 65 | 70 | 180 | 250 |
| Sessions per month | 8 | 2 | 4 | 1 |
| Paid attendees per session (Year 3) | 13 | 15 | 11 | 14 |
| Organiser takings per month | 6,760 | 2,100 | 7,920 | 3,500 |
| Ryze fee per month | 649 | 202 | 760 | 403 |
| Months to pay back onboarding | 4 | 7 | 4 | — |
| Lifetime contribution per community | 17,900 | 3,000 | 15,200 | 400 per event |

What this tells us:

- **Body is the engine.** Over half of GMV, the most frequent sessions, and the clubs already exist.
- **Soul earns the most per community.** Prices are higher because they include ingredients and materials. Groups are small, and they make the best photos and stories.
- **Mind earns little on its own** (AED 202 a month per book club). It is worth having because it brings in people who don't do sport, and it works best as a cheap way in to the other pillars. Two options to test: a monthly pass (for example AED 150 for two meetings plus a discount at a Soul event), or pairing with partner cafes that pay to host.

Free communities stay free. Many run clubs never charge. They stay listed because they bring members in, and the forecast does not count them.

## 5. How Ryze makes money

| Stream | How it works | Share of 2029 revenue |
|---|---|---:|
| Booking fees: Body, Mind, Soul | 10% of each paid booking, after a 3-month fee-free start. No fee is kept on refunds | 64% |
| Workshop fees | 12% of tickets for one-off workshops and events, which Ryze promotes to all members | 6% |
| Mind Body Soul days | Ryze sells the tickets (AED 295) and pays the costs (AED 170 a guest plus AED 8,000 a day) | 13% |
| Ryze Pro | AED 149 a month per community, from January 2028: monthly passes, waitlists, payout reports, co-hosts (from `parked.md`) | 5% |
| Venue partnerships | Cafes, kitchens and studios pay to host communities or for a featured venue slot. No ads and no supplements (D3) | 12% |

**Payments.** Members pay through the organiser's own Ziina account (individuals and freelance coaches, sign-up with Emirates ID) or Stripe account (clubs with a trade licence). Ryze takes its fee from each payment. The organiser pays the card fee, about 2.6% + AED 1 on Ziina. The forecast lets you test Ryze paying it instead (Assumptions: "Share of processing that Ryze pays").

**VAT.** Ryze registers once its own taxable income passes AED 375,000 in 12 months, which happens in 2028. From then on it adds 5% VAT to its fee. Organisers handle VAT on their own sales.

## 6. Go-to-market

Supply first. A member who finds three Pilates groups and no book club leaves.

| Phase | When | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paid pilot** | Jan to Mar 2027 | 16 paid communities live (10 Body, 3 Mind, 3 Soul); first payouts | Convert the 10 clubs that already price sessions in the prototype. Founder recruits 3 book club hosts and 3 Soul hosts. 0% fee |
| **Fill the pillars** | Apr to Dec 2027 | 90 paid communities, 6 workshops a month | Community lead recruits organisers from Instagram and WhatsApp. Organisers bring their own members with invite links and QR codes (D35). First Mind Body Soul day in June |
| **Prove the economics** | 2028 | 180 communities, Ryze Pro live, first partners | Launch Ryze Pro. Sign venue partners. Hire an events producer. Raise seed on proof |
| **Scale the UAE** | 2029 | 275+ communities, Abu Dhabi at depth | Abu Dhabi already has clubs in the prototype. Repeat the playbook area by area |

Growth comes mostly from organisers. Each organiser brings an existing WhatsApp group onto Ryze, and those members then find other communities. Paid marketing stays modest: 10k a month in 2027, 20k in 2028 and 35k in 2029.

### What moves the numbers

Each line changes one input from the base case.

| Change | Funding needed | EBITDA, Dec 2029 | First profitable month |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Base | 2.50M | (24k) | Early 2030 |
| No fee-free months (0% start removed) | 1.84M | 3k | Dec 2029 |
| Fee of 12% on community bookings | 2.08M | 2k | Dec 2029 |
| Community churn 2% a month, not 3–4% | 2.11M | 7k | Dec 2029 |
| Two more paid attendees per Body session | 2.29M | (12k) | Early 2030 |
| Soul price AED 220, not 180 | 2.36M | (15k) | Early 2030 |
| Ryze Pro adoption 25% / 40%, not 15% / 25% | 2.42M | (18k) | Early 2030 |
| 12% fee + 2% churn + higher Pro adoption | 1.61M | 47k | Jun 2029 |

**The fee-free start is the most expensive single decision.** Because the network is growing, a large share of communities is always in its first 3 months. Over three years the 0% period costs about AED 670k. A cheaper way to win organisers: 0% on their first AED 2,000 of bookings, or for the first month only.

**Retention of communities matters more than retention of members.** A community that stops takes its members with it. The curator's job (D18) and the organiser's five-minute week (D30) protect revenue directly.

### Not in the model

- **Summer.** Outdoor Body sessions drop from June to September. Indoor sessions, Mind and Soul hold up better, and that is a reason to have them. The forecast uses flat months, so summer 2027 will run below it.
- **Corporate tax.** Shown at 9% above AED 375,000 of profit, with no carry-forward of earlier losses. Ryze has no taxable profit in the forecast period.
- **Free communities and members.** They drive growth, but the forecast only counts paid bookings.

## 9. Funding

Base case: **AED 2.5M (USD 680k)** to reach break-even, with the cash low point in December 2029. The model's opening cash is a placeholder of AED 750k.

Two rounds:

1. **Pre-seed, now: AED 750k to 1M (USD 200k to 270k).** Pays for the backend and payments build, the paid pilot and the first community lead. Lasts to about Q4 2027.
2. **Seed, Q4 2027: AED 1.8M to 2.2M (USD 500k to 600k)**, raised on proof:
   - 80+ paid communities across all three pillars
   - AED 350k+ GMV a month, growing (base case: about 380k to 450k in Q4 2027)
   - 35% of first-time bookers book again within 14 days
   - Organiser churn under 4% a month

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Organisers take payments off Ryze to avoid the 10% fee | Lost revenue | Make booking through Ryze the easy path: paid sign-up lists, no-show tracking (D26), payout reports, streaks only count booked sessions. Ryze Pro adds value organisers can't get from WhatsApp |
| Community churn above 4% a month | Funding need rises fast | Weekly organiser summary (D31), curator check-ins, co-organisers (D32) so a club survives one person leaving |
| **Ryze-run events need permits** | Fines; stops the flagship days | Dubai sports events need a Dubai Sports Council permit and insurance (Executive Council Resolution 1 of 2020). Food events need food safety approval. Take legal advice before the first Mind Body Soul day in June 2027. Communities and workshops are still run by their organisers |
| Cooking and food workshops | Food safety incidents | List only hosts with a licensed kitchen or venue. Show allergen notes on Soul sessions |
| Refunds, chargebacks and disputes | Cost and trust | Clear refund rule per session, shown before payment. Organiser sets it; Ryze keeps no fee on refunds |
| Payment provider terms | Ziina or Stripe may limit platform fees | Confirm how Ziina takes a platform fee before the build (the open question in `parked.md`). Keep both providers |
| Personal data (PDPL) | Fines; loss of trust | D37 safeguards: consent, 18+, UAE or PDPL-level hosting. Privacy policy, terms and delete-account before taking real payments (`parked.md`) |
| Summer | Lower Body GMV for three or four months | Push indoor, Mind and Soul sessions in summer |

## 11. What the paid pilot must prove (Jan to Mar 2027)

| Question | Signal |
|---|---|
| Will members pay in the app? | 70%+ of bookings to paid communities are paid through Ryze, not at the session |
| Will organisers stay once the fee starts? | 80%+ of pilot communities still live a month after their fee-free period ends |
| Do people come back? | 35%+ of first-time bookers book again within 14 days |
| Do Mind and Soul find an audience? | 5 of the 6 Mind and Soul communities fill at least 60% of places |
| Do the pillars cross over? | 15%+ of members book in a second pillar within 60 days |

These replace the free-pilot signals in `concept-verdict.md` once payments are live.

## 12. Assumptions to check first

Marked yellow in the forecast. Check them in this order, because they move the result most:

1. **Community churn** (3–4% a month). Ask the 10 pilot clubs how long they have run and how many similar groups they have seen stop.
2. **New paid communities per month** (8 in 2027 across all pillars). Count book clubs, supper clubs and art groups active on Instagram in Dubai.
3. **Fee-free start.** Ask five organisers whether 0% for the first month, or for the first AED 2,000, would still win them over.
4. **Mind pricing.** Ask five book club hosts what they charge and what members would pay for a monthly pass.
5. **Soul prices and group sizes.** Check 10 live cooking and pottery listings in Dubai.
6. **Ziina platform fee mechanics and card costs.** Confirm with Ziina and Stripe.
