# Ryze Club business plan

*Find your people, keep showing up.* For mind, body and soul.

All money is in AED and excludes VAT. USD is at the pegged rate of 3.6725. Every number comes from `ryze-club-forecast.xlsx` (base scenario) unless a source is named. Change the blue cells on its **Assumptions** tab and every table updates.

**Rebuilt on 22 November 2026.** This version follows the decisions recorded in the decision log up to D52, including those made in other conversations (section 2). It starts when the seed round closes. The MVP and its own small round come before it and are not modelled.

## 1. Summary

Ryze Club is where people in Dubai find a community to belong to, then keep showing up. It covers three pillars:

| Pillar | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Mind** | Book clubs, language exchange, writing circles, board games, talks |
| **Body** | Running, padel, football, basketball, tennis, boxing, Pilates, yoga |
| **Soul** | Supper clubs, cooking, pottery, calligraphy, photography walks, music, dance |

Free clubs stay free. Hosts who charge set their own price, members pay in the app, and **Ryze keeps 10% of the host's price** (D47). Members and friends can also run private games and events, such as 5-a-side, games nights and brunches, where Ryze adds **AED 3 per guest** (D48).

**Base case, three years after the seed (July 2027 to June 2030):**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Paid hosts live (year end) | 90 | 178 | 276 |
| Value paid to hosts through Ryze (GMV) | 3.25M | 9.32M | 16.93M |
| Ryze revenue | 316k | 978k | 1.86M |
| Total costs | 1.64M | 2.33M | 2.62M |
| Profit or loss | (1.33M) | (1.35M) | (754k) |

- **Seed round: about AED 2.6M (USD 700k).** That covers 18 months of losses plus a 25% buffer.
- **Booking fees cover the founder's pay from February 2028**, eight months after the seed.
- **Not yet profitable within 36 months in the base case.** Losses fall to about 40k a month by mid-2030. The high case is profitable from November 2029.
- **A lean team carries the seed all the way.** Hire the second engineer and host support only when growth is on the high-case track. That lowers total losses to about AED 2.4M, which fits inside a 2.6M seed.

## 3. The problem

Dubai is a city of newcomers looking for their people. More than 3 million people took part in the Dubai Fitness Challenge in 2025 (WAM). One local writer describes residents "craving connection" and puts paid community events at AED 300 to 400 (*Half-Light*, p. 53).

Finding a group is still hard. Clubs live across Instagram, WhatsApp and one-off websites. Hosts chase payments by bank transfer and lose track of who paid. Book clubs, supper clubs and dance socials have no Playtomic.

- **For members:** one place to find a group, see who's going, pay in one tap, and come back.
- **For hosts:** a page, bookings that arrive paid into their own Ziina account, and no chasing. A routine week takes under five minutes (D30).

- **Body is the engine:** 60% of GMV, with frequent sessions, and the clubs already exist.
- **Soul earns almost as much per host**, from fewer, pricier sessions.
- **Mind earns little on its own.** It brings in people who don't do sport and leads them to the other pillars.
- **Private events earn little each** (AED 24 an event), but every invite brings new members.

## 5. How Ryze makes money (D47)

| Stream | How | Share of Year 3 revenue |
|---|---|---:|
| Club sessions (Body, Mind, Soul) | 10% of the host's price, taken from the host's share | 81% |
| Workshops | 10%, like any session | 6% |
| Private games and events | AED 3 per guest, on top of their share | 3% |
| Venue commissions | Venues pay for the regular groups Ryze brings (parked idea 6) | 10% |

Members pay through Ziina straight into the host's account. Ryze never holds session money. The host pays the card fee. `money-flows.md` shows every type of club.

**VAT:** Ryze registers once its own income passes AED 375,000 in 12 months (early in Year 2), then adds 5% VAT to its fee.

### Ambitious case: 300 hosts in Year 2, 1,000 in Year 3

Year 1 is the same as the base case (90 hosts). To reach 300 and then 1,000 paid hosts, Ryze must sign up about **24 new hosts a month in Year 2 and 81 a month in Year 3**, against 8 to 16 in the base case. That can't be done with the base team, so this case adds hires. They are on the Assumptions tab as "Scale-up" roles, set to not hired (99) by default:

- **Five more community leads** (months 13, 16, 25, 27, 29). Each signs up about 10 to 15 hosts a month.
- **An Abu Dhabi city lead** (month 19).
- **Two more host support people** (months 25, 31).
- **A third engineer** (month 25).
- **Marketing** of AED 40k a month in Year 2 and 90k in Year 3.

| AED | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Paid hosts (year end) | 90 | 299 | 998 |
| Paid bookings | 40k | 170k | 609k |
| GMV | 3.25M | 13.6M | 48.3M |
| Ryze revenue | 316k | 1.39M | 4.87M |
| Team | 1.16M | 1.99M | 3.10M |
| Marketing and onboarding | 238k | 654k | 1.66M |
| Total costs | 1.64M | 3.05M | 5.49M |
| **Profit or loss** | **(1.33M)** | **(1.66M)** | **(621k)** |

- **Profitable every month from March 2030** (month 33). By June 2030 Ryze earns about AED 566k a month and makes about 84k a month.
- **Total losses: AED 3.79M**, with the low point in February 2030.
- **An AED 2.8M seed runs out around May 2029** (month 23). That fits a Series A at the end of Year 2, raised on about 300 hosts and AED 1.4M of revenue, growing more than 3x a year. On 8x to 15x the next year's revenue, Ryze would then be worth about **AED 39M to 73M (USD 11M to 20M)**.

**What has to be true:**

- **Seven community people each sign up 10 to 15 hosts a month**, all year.
- **Host churn stays at 3–4%.** At 5% a month, Ryze reaches 886 hosts and total losses rise to AED 4.2M.
- **Attendance holds.** At 20% lower attendance, total losses rise to AED 4.8M and Ryze is not profitable by June 2030.
- **Dubai and Abu Dhabi have enough paid hosts.** 1,000 is a large share of the UAE's paid community clubs, studios and classes. Count them before promising this to investors.

If the base team tried to reach 1,000 hosts without these hires, the forecast would look profitable from September 2029. That isn't realistic, because the same three community people can't sign up 81 hosts a month.

### What moves the numbers

| Change | Total losses | Seed (18 months + buffer) | Profit or loss, Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base | 3.43M | 2.58M | (754k) |
| Lean team: no second engineer or host support | **2.43M** | 2.19M | (299k) |
| Host churn 2% a month, not 3–4% | 3.09M | 2.52M | (514k) |
| Fee 12% instead of 10% | 2.87M | 2.40M | (429k) |
| Coaching switched on (parked, 15%) | 2.73M | 2.45M | (236k) |
| **Three fee-free months for new hosts** | **4.05M** | 2.84M | (1.06M) |

- **Hiring is the biggest lever.** The team is 60% to 71% of costs.
- **Fee-free months are expensive.** Because Ryze keeps adding hosts, many are always in their free months. Decide this before launch.
- **Keeping hosts matters more than keeping members.** A host who stops takes their members with them.

### Not in the model

- **Summer:** outdoor Body sessions drop from June to September. Indoor, Mind and Soul sessions hold up better.
- **Free clubs:** they bring members in but earn nothing, so they are not counted.
- **Corporate tax:** 9% above AED 375,000 of profit. There is no taxable profit in the period.

## 9. Seed round, ownership and value

**Raise about AED 2.6M (USD 700k)** at about USD 3.5M pre-money, in July 2027, on the pilot's numbers.

**Recommended:** start with the lean team, and hire the second engineer and host support only once growth runs ahead of the base case. On the lean plan, total losses (AED 2.43M) fit inside the seed with its buffer, so no further raise is needed to reach month 36. With the full team, the seed runs out around May 2029 (month 23), and another AED 0.85M or so is needed.

| Method | AED | USD |
|---|---:|---:|

**Warning:** on the base case, Ryze a year after the seed is worth about the same as, or less than, the seed post-money of USD 4.2M. A higher-priced next round needs the high case. That is another reason to run lean and aim to reach profit on the seed alone.

Sources for the valuation ranges are in the Valuation tab notes: Clear.world, the JADA VC Valuation Report (February 2026), Flippa and 2026 MENA deals. The revenue multiples are assumptions to check against stage-matched deals.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Hosts won't accept 10% and ask members to pay in cash | Ask the 15 pilot hosts before launch (PRFAQ). Keep booking through Ryze the easy path: paid lists, no chasing, members' streaks |
| Ziina can't split payments, so Ryze must invoice hosts | Confirm with Ziina. A monthly invoice risks late payment |
| Host churn above 4% a month | Five-minute week (D30), co-organisers (D32), curator check-ins |
| Ryze holds money or organises events by accident | Never hold session money (D47). Private events stay invite-only (D40). No Ryze-run ticketed events without legal advice |
| Data protection (PDPL) | D37 safeguards; UAE hosting (D38); a check of reliability records before launch (D50) |
| A down round a year after the seed | Run lean and aim for profit on the seed (section 9) |
