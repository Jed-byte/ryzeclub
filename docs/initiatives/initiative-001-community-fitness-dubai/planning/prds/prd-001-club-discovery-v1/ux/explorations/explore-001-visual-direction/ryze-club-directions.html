Three ways Ryze Club could look and feel

Each direction puts a different thing first: the next session, the photos, or the streak. That choice shapes the whole product, so the colours and type matter less than what leads.

Clubs, people, times and numbers below are examples. Coloured blocks stand in for members' photos.

Direction A

Timetable

The next session leads. Every club page opens on a ticket with the time, place, pace and one button. It reads like a departures board: fast, factual, calm.

StrengthsThe quickest path to an RSVP. Works even when a club has no photos yet. Easiest to keep accurate.
WeaknessesFeels like a utility. Shows little of the people or the atmosphere, which is what convinces a nervous newcomer.
Direction B

Album

The photos lead. A club page opens on pictures from recent sessions, with the next session held in a bar at the bottom of the screen. Members' posts are the design.

StrengthsShows the people, which is what makes a newcomer feel safe to turn up. Rewards members for posting. The share card spreads the name for free.
WeaknessesLooks empty for a club with no photos, so each club needs a few before launch. Photo loading must be fast on mobile data.
Direction C

Scoreboard

The streak leads. A member opens the site to their week: the streak number, the days they've shown up, and what's left before Sunday. Club pages show how active the club is.

StrengthsThe strongest pull for existing members to come back. Sets up future rewards naturally. Standings by weeks attended let beginners appear.
WeaknessesA first-time visitor has no streak, so the lead element is empty for them. A competitive tone can put off the nervous newcomer the product is for.

Recommendation: Album as the base, with two borrowed parts

  • Public club pages follow Album. They are what people land on from Instagram, and photos of real members answer "are these my kind of people?"
  • The session bar borrows Timetable's ticket. Time, place, pace and one button, always visible.
  • The signed-in home borrows Scoreboard's week view, in Album's lighter look, so the streak leads only for people who have one.

Status: exploratory. Nothing here is final until a direction is chosen.
